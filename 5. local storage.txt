*Local storage

-Local storage allows a website to save data on the user's computer or phone.

-that data stays saved even after close the browser.

-Why do we use Local Storage?
	We use it to save information such as:
	- User preferences
	- Shopping cart items
	- To-do list tasks
	- Username or other non-sensitive settings
	- Game scores

-important Local Storage methods
	-localStorage.setItem("username", "Alex");
	-localStorage.getItem("username");
	-localStorage.removeItem("username");
	-localStorage.clear(); //clear all
